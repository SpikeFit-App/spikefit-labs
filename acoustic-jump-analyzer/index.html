The Gravity Math

Once you break contact with the earth, you are a projectile governed strictly by gravity ($g \approx 9.80665 \text{ m/s}^2$). Because ascent time equals descent time, we can determine the apex of your vertical jump purely using the time elapsed in the air ($t$):

Kinematic Trajectory Equation
$$Height = \frac{1}{8} g t^2$$
Inches Formula: $Height = 48.27 \times t^2$
Metric Formula: $Height = 122.6 \times t^2$
