How to film

  • Film from the side, square to the approach path. Prop the phone up so it doesn't move.
  • Keep the whole body in frame, head to feet, from your ready stance through the landing.
  • Use slow motion if your phone has it. Fast steps blur at normal speed.
  • One athlete in frame. Trim long clips with Set Start and Set End.
